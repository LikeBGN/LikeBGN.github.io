About

I am currently conducting research at the IRMS Lab, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). My research interests focus on cross-embodiment generalization and humanoid robot manipulation.

I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen, in July 2025. From June 2025 to May 2026, I worked on underwater visual perception at LIAS, The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Junfeng Wu.
